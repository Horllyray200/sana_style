WHAT YOU'LL LEARN

Three skills. One intensive week.

Designed to help you understand the construction behind structured garments — not just copy a finished look.

01

Introduction to Bustier Pattern Drafting

Build your foundation for drafting a well-structured bustier pattern and understand the measurements and shaping that make the fit work.

  • Pattern-drafting foundation
  • Measurements & shaping
  • Understanding structure
02

Bustier Caging

Learn the role of internal structure and how caging can support the shape and finish of a structured bustier.

  • Cage placement
  • Structural support
  • Clean finishing principles
03

Hip Padding

Understand how to create and position hip padding for a balanced silhouette and a polished garment shape.

  • Padding construction
  • Placement & balance
  • Finishing for a clean result
